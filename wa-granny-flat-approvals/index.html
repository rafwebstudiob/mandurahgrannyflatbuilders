WA granny flat approvals in Mandurah

In short: In Western Australia, a granny flat is an ancillary dwelling under the R-Codes, generally up to 70 m². Most designs that meet the R-Codes can be exempt from a separate planning application, but a building permit is still required.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page summarises official Western Australian planning information so you know what to expect. It is not legal or planning advice. Your builder, a building surveyor or the City of Mandurah can confirm what applies to your lot.

What counts as a granny flat in Western Australia

The Residential Design Codes (R-Codes, State Planning Policy 7.3) call a granny flat an ancillary dwelling: a self-contained dwelling associated with a single house or grouped dwelling on the same site. The deemed-to-comply provisions allow one ancillary dwelling, generally with an internal floor area of no more than 70 m². Amendments that took effect in April 2024 also mean that most ancillary dwellings which meet the R-Codes standards can be exempt from a separate planning application.

Sources: Western Australian Planning Commission, State Planning Policy 7.3 Residential Design Codes; Government of Western Australia, Granny flat exemptions commence to boost housing diversity (9 April 2024).

Building approval is still needed

Even when a planning application is not required, a building permit from the local government permit authority is still required to build or convert an ancillary dwelling. Other things can still add a step, including bushfire-prone mapping, coastal or flood controls, heritage, and estate covenants or strata rules. Check the City of Mandurah planning information for your address, or ask the builder or a registered building surveyor to do that early.

Local council: Mandurah

Your local council is City of Mandurah. Check the council's planning information for your address (zoning, overlays, bush fire, flood or heritage mapping), as these can change what is allowed for a granny flat. Your builder or certifier normally does this check as part of the quote.

Some Mandurah properties are on canal estates or close to the estuary or the coast, and parts of the district are mapped as bushfire prone. Check the City of Mandurah planning information for flood, coastal and bushfire mapping, and any estate covenants, before you settle on a design.

Builders, contracts and insurance

In Western Australia, the Home Building Contracts Act 1991 generally applies to fixed-price home building or associated work contracts between $7,500 and $500,000. For residential building work valued at more than $20,000, a registered builder must take out home indemnity insurance in the owner's name and give you the certificate before taking any payment or starting work. A registered building contractor is required when the work needs a building permit, is valued at $20,000 or more (or $50,000 or more for a Class 10a building such as a shed or garage), and is in the prescribed area of the State, unless an exemption applies. You can check a builder's registration with Building and Energy.

Sources: Building and Energy, Building or renovating your home; Building and Energy, Do I need to be a registered building contractor?; Building and Energy, Home indemnity insurance fact sheet.
