Building a granny flat in Greenfields

Greenfields is an established inland suburb east of the city, with later family homes on suburban blocks.

Post-war and 1980s homes often sit towards the front of the block, leaving a backyard that may suit a detached granny flat.

Level blocks usually make slab construction and machine access simpler.
